# Journas

A shared travel planner that connects places, time, and tasks for each day of a trip.

## The Unique Kernel

Each date has its own connected map, vertical schedule, and task list. A place's identity carries into scheduled visits, tasks can link to either, and collaborators edit the same trip live while keeping their own workspace layout.

## Who It's For

Travellers organising a multi-day holiday with friends or family who otherwise split their itinerary across a map, calendar, and checklist.

## The Core Loop

Sign in, create or join a trip, select a date, pin places, schedule visits, and link preparation tasks. Return to the saved day, change the layout to suit the work, and see collaborators' updates without refreshing.

## Inspiration & Identity

Compact English interface, purple default accent with a personal colour setting. Schedule hours are arranged vertically, with a text-free current-time line. Map and schedule share a resizable split; tasks float above them.

## Why This Matters to the Learner

The creator requested a complete, usable holiday planner and a Build With AI: Basics submission. Personal motivation and learning reflections have not been supplied and must not be invented.

## What "Working" Looks Like

Two signed-in accounts join one trip. One adds a place, visit, and linked task; the other sees the edit live. Changing date and reopening restores the day's plan and each person's layout. Removing a shared trip from one account preserves it for the other; an owner's explicit manual deletion removes it for everyone.

## The POC Boundary

The approved user scope includes authentication, the complete linked daily workspace, live sharing, settings, sparse server persistence, retention, trip limits, deployment, and submission materials. The creator explicitly authorised this broader build; the skill pack's suggested 2–4-hour PoC is a teaching guideline rather than an agreed product cut.

## Later

No additional product features have been agreed.

## Explicitly Cut

Bookings, payments, route optimisation, AI-generated itineraries, messaging, and commercial upsells are outside the requested scope. No approved planner requirement is deferred.

## Approval Provenance

The creator approved the displayed plan through “PLEASE IMPLEMENT THIS PLAN”, including automatic personal removal after one year and owner-only global manual deletion.
