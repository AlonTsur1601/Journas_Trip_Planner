Journas

Privacy policy

Updated October 6, 2026. Journas is a collaborative trip planner. For privacy questions and requests, contact journas.app@gmail.com.

Information used by Journas

We use your account identifier, email address, and, when you sign in with Google, the basic profile information provided by Google, including your name and profile picture. You can choose a separate name and picture in Journas. We store your trips, places, notes, schedules, tasks, sharing permissions, and preferences to provide the planner.

Sign-in and Google data

Google sign-in is used to authenticate your account. Journas does not request access to your Gmail messages, Google Drive files, or contacts. Email and password sign-in is managed by Firebase Authentication; passwords are not stored in trip documents. Your Google profile information is used for account identity and collaboration, not advertising.

Sharing and service providers

Travelers you add to a trip can access its shared plan according to their permissions. Anyone with an active public sharing link may access the preview allowed by that link. Vercel hosts the application, and Google Firebase provides authentication and database storage. Account verification emails are delivered through the project Gmail account. OpenFreeMap supplies map tiles, and Photon by Komoot processes place searches and reverse-location searches. These providers receive information needed for their services, such as requests, search text, coordinates, or network addresses.

Browser storage and security

Journas uses browser storage for sign-in sessions, screen restoration, and workspace layout. Server-side records limit request rates; network addresses used for public-preview rate limits are hashed. We use encrypted network connections and access controls to protect account and trip data.

Retention and your choices

Account and planner information is retained to provide your saved workspace. Trip cleanup follows the automatic deletion preferences available in Settings. You can edit your profile and plans, manage trip access and sharing links, and change your retention preferences. Contact the project email above to request assistance with access or deletion of your account information. Signing out does not delete saved trips.

Changes

This policy may be updated when Journas changes how it handles information. The date above identifies the latest version.
