Journas

Isolated testing environment

This Journas version is for authorized testing. Payments use Paddle Sandbox and do not charge real money. Advertisement placeholders do not serve real ads or track ad interactions.

Plans

Tourist is free. Traveler is $4.99 monthly or $49.99 yearly. Adventurer is $9.99 monthly or $99.99 yearly. Annual prices are billed once per year. Applicable taxes are displayed before checkout. Production sales remain disabled until the responsible adult completes provider approval and the final sales terms.

Cancellation and data

Cancellation prevents renewal; paid access continues until the end of the paid period. Downgrades may make excess trips and premium content read-only. Retention limits are 90 days after a trip for tourist, 365 days for traveler, or unlimited for adventurer unless a shorter setting is selected. An owner's deletion removes a shared trip for all travelers.

Do not enter sensitive personal data into test trips. Contact journas.app@gmail.com for assistance.
