## References compared for every symbol

Compared Lucide, Tabler, Phosphor regular and Google Material Icons at 40px and 19px. Material references inform recognition; no Material geometry is embedded. Waterfall uses waves as a water analogue, bridge uses architecture as a structural analogue, and camping uses holiday_village as a lodging analogue. These are not claimed to be identical concepts.
